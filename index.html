    How the matching works
    1. Clean. Both documents are lowercased and stripped of accents and punctuation.
    2. Window overlap. The manuscript is read in overlapping 13-word windows and each source in 11-word windows, one word at a time across the whole PDF. When a pair shares 8 or more distinct words, in any order, those manuscript words are marked.
    3. Phrase overlap. With common words removed, every 3-word phrase is compared. A source sharing at least 21 phrases also gets credit for phrases that only it contains.
    4. Score. The similarity is the share of manuscript words marked by the sources you leave switched on.

    It finds copied and lightly reworded text, not paraphrase. A match is a prompt to look, not a verdict.
